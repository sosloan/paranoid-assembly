THE FIRST THOUGHT

Meanwhile, here's your Ada-only drill:

begin end. declare begin. is null. loop exit.

Practice makes perfect. 🎯

READING ORDER (THE CATCH)

"EXIT LOOP? NULL ID? BEGIN DECLARE? END BEGIN?"

THE CORRECTION

Good catch — order matters. Corrections:

  DECLARE BEGIN END   (not begin declare)
  LOOP EXIT END LOOP  (exit lives inside the loop)
  NULL                — no subject needed; null; is a complete statement
  BEGIN END           — never end begin

MINIMAL VALID ADA, RESERVED WORDS ONLY

practice.adb

  declare
  begin
     null;
  end;

practice2.adb

  begin
     loop
        exit;
     end loop;
  end;

practice3.adb

  begin
     begin
        null;
     exception
        when others =>
           null;
     end;
  end;

  declare
