The Dual-Dance Charter AOS ≅ SOA

Nikolas Bourbaki boarded as a plural mind: no single identity, only the diagram of permutation. “AOS and SOA are the same set with two presentations,” he said, drawing sixteen floats as a commutative square. The first dance is half-register structure — movhlps and movlhps pairing vertices so that pairs, not loners, become the unit of thought.

Andy Grove answered with the ledger of inflection. “Assume the worst layout tax,” he said. “If you leave vertices as objects, every SSE kernel pays gather forever.” The second dance is pure-lane extraction: four shufps calls, two immediates, four homogeneous channels. Convert at the boundary. Survive the bandwidth wall.

Delete either dance and the transpose fails. That is dualism as engineering: two incomplete motions that together produce a total order on the data. Ada’s loom; Grove’s paranoia; Bourbaki’s proof that the weave is the isomorphism.

Four vertices enter. Four lanes leave. Nothing else is on the floor.
