The Tri-Partner Charter range · rate · funds

Nikolas Bourbaki boarded with a commutative diagram of prices. “A fill is legal only when the band is a non-empty closed interval on the tick lattice,” he said, drawing PRICE_MIN…PRICE_MAX as the ambient book. Dance I is pure structure: market_on_tick, market_in_range, market_band_ok.

Andy Grove answered with the inflection of the exchange levy. “Assume a bad rate arrives,” he said. “Gate it before you write net. Collect VAT by subtraction so no penny evaporates under wrap.” Dance II is the levy: market_rate_ok, market_levy — ST_RATE or ST_RANGE before any handshake.

The Posted Stall holds the third position. No haggle. The list price is the list price. After band and levy clear, the wallet must cover gross. Dance III is market_quote_fixed composing the gates and writing Q_STATUS. Three incomplete motions; one total order on a fill.

Delete any partner and the stall either over-clears, under-taxes, or spends a short wallet. That is tri-partner engineering: band, levy, funds — and only the paranoid survive.
